# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include respectful communication, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, public or private abuse, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report concerns privately to the maintainers.
